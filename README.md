## Featured Projects

### Project 1 — Tech Layoffs Workforce Impact Analysis (2020–2026)
**SQL · PostgreSQL · Tableau Public**

End-to-end analysis of 1,780+ global tech layoff events across 50+ countries. Built a complete data pipeline in PostgreSQL and delivered a dual-dashboard Tableau workbook with KPI cards, treemap, scatter plot, and world map, all connected to dynamic filters.

**Key finding:** Post-IPO companies accounted for the largest share of layoffs despite having the most access to capital.

➡ [View Project](https://github.com/Khadejia/data-analyst-portfolio/tree/main/Tech-Layoffs-Workforce-Analysis)

---

### Project 2 — NYC Restaurant Health Inspections (2022–2026)
**SQL · PostgreSQL · Tableau Public**

Public health analysis of 400,000+ NYC Department of Health inspection records across all five boroughs. Built a 5-dashboard Tableau workbook with a custom dark green sidebar, tab navigation, grade distribution KPIs, and a restaurant records view with wildcard search.

**Key finding:** Critical violations are more common than letter grades suggest, a restaurant can earn Grade A while still having critical violations on record.

➡ [View Project](https://github.com/Khadejia/data-analyst-portfolio/tree/main/NYC-Restaurant-Inspections)

---

### Project 3 — Netflix Content Strategy Analysis (2016–2021)
**SQL · PostgreSQL · Tableau Public**

Catalog analysis of 8,800+ Netflix titles examining genre strategy, global production trends, and rating distribution. Built a single dark-theme Tableau dashboard inspired by the Netflix interface with a content growth timeline, world map, top directors lollipop chart, and genre area chart.

**Key finding:** Dramas dominate the catalog and the US leads production, but India and South Korea have grown significantly as global content hubs.

➡ [View Project](https://github.com/Khadejia/data-analyst-portfolio/tree/main/Netflix-Content-Strategy-Analysis)
